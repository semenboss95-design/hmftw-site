Where the numbers come from

  • Sold for (hammer): not yet. It is to come from public auction-result archives, the winning bid without fees, only results with a final sold status; no archive has passed our checks so far.
  • Retail estimate: the auction's own estimate printed next to each lot, collected nightly from the public Copart search for lots whose sale date has passed.
  • Last visible bid: the last bid we saw on the lot in our nightly collection.
  • Private sellers and dealers: not yet. No listings source has cleared our legal check, so nothing is read and nothing is stored.

What they are not

  • A hammer is not what the car costs you: fees, broker, storage, transport, title and repair come on top.
  • A retail estimate is the auction's own figure, not a sale and not our estimate.
  • A bid is not a sale: the lot may not have sold, and where it did, the hammer can be higher.
  • An asking price is not a sale price.
