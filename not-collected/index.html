A model of a make that is not in its row we don't track: an empty result for it is about the border of our observation, not about such cars being absent at auction.

Why the list is so short

Every model spelling is a separate request to the source catalog, and we make few requests a day on purpose. The list grows by hand, and until then a make missing from the site says something only about us.
